7. RENEWAL AND NOTICE
If Tenant desires to renew or extend this lease for a new term, Tenant shall give Landlord written notice of such intent [[NOTICE:quantified]]not less than {{notice_days_text}} prior to the expiration of the Term[[/NOTICE]][[NOTICE:ambiguous]]a reasonable period of time prior to the expiration of the Term[[/NOTICE]]. 8. LATE CHARGES
If Landlord does not receive the full Rent by {{late_grace_days}} day(s) after its due date, Tenant shall pay a late charge of {{late_fee_text}}. Landlord's acceptance of a late charge shall not constitute a waiver of any default.

9. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement at the end of the Term by giving the notice required by Section 7. Tenant shall surrender the Premises in good condition, ordinary wear and tear excepted, remove all personal property, and return all keys to Landlord on or before the last day of the Term.

10. UTILITIES AND MAINTENANCE
Tenant shall pay for all utilities supplied to the Premises during the Term, including electricity, gas, water, sewer, trash, internet, and any other services Tenant uses. Tenant shall keep the Premises clean and sanitary and shall promptly notify Landlord of any condition requiring repair. Landlord shall maintain the structure, roof, walls, and major systems in good repair.

11. INSURANCE
Tenant shall maintain throughout the Term a renter's insurance policy covering Tenant's personal property and liability, with Landlord named as an interested party, and shall provide proof of coverage upon Landlord's request.

12. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the state in which the Premises are located. Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall first be submitted to good-faith negotiation between the parties.

13. ENTIRE AGREEMENT
This Agreement, including any attachments, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior understandings. No modification is effective unless in writing and signed by both parties.
